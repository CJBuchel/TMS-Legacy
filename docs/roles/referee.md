# I am a referee

You score matches at your table on a tablet. TMS shows you the right team, works out the score as you answer, checks the sheet for mistakes and sends it in.

!!! abstract "Your screen"
    **Referee Scoring**.

## Getting ready

1. Open TMS on your tablet and log in with the referee account you were given.
2. From the home screen open **Referee Scoring**.
3. In the header, select **your table**.
4. Enter **your name** as the referee.

That is all the setup there is. Leave Referee Scoring open: while it is open with a table selected, the head referee can see that your table is staffed.

## The scoring screen

The header across the top shows:

- your **table**,
- the **team** you are about to score,
- the **match** number and how many matches there are, and
- the **round**.

The team, match and round fill in by themselves from the schedule once the head referee loads a match. You do not choose them.

Below the header is the scoresheet, one block per mission. At the bottom are the running **score** and three buttons: **No Show**, **Clear** and **Submit**.

## Scoring a match

1. Wait for the match to finish.
2. Go through the scoresheet **with the team**, answering each question.
    - Yes/No and multiple choice questions are buttons. Select the answer.
    - Questions with a large number range show a counter. Use **+1** and **−1**, or **+5** and **−5**, to reach the number.
3. Watch the **score** in the bottom corner update as you go.
4. Set **Gracious Professionalism**. It defaults to *3 - Accomplished*.
5. Add a **private comment** if you need to. This is only seen by event staff.
6. Select **Submit** and confirm.

After you submit, the sheet resets and the tablet waits for your next team.

!!! tip
    After you answer a question the page moves on to the next one for you. Counter questions stay where they are so you can keep tapping.

## When the sheet will not submit

The scoresheet checks itself. If an answer is impossible, a message appears in red under the question and **Submit** is blocked until it is fixed.

Examples:

- a bonus is claimed without the thing it depends on,
- more items are counted in a scoring area than exist on the field, or
- a question has no answer.

Fix the highlighted answer and submit again. The message tells you which rule was broken.

!!! info "A comment is required for some GP ratings"
    If Gracious Professionalism is anything other than *3 - Accomplished*, you must enter a private comment explaining why before you can submit.

## A team did not turn up

Select **No Show** and confirm. This records the round for that team with a score of zero and marks it as a no-show, so the event does not show the score as missing.

## Starting again

**Clear** resets every answer to its default. Use it if you started scoring the wrong team's run. It cannot be undone.

## If you make a mistake after submitting

You cannot change a scoresheet once it is submitted. Tell the head referee or judge advisor straight away, with the team number and round. The judge advisor can correct or delete the sheet.

Do not submit a second sheet for the same team and round to "fix" the first. That creates a conflict that someone then has to resolve.

## Common situations

??? question "My tablet shows the wrong team"
    Check the table in the header is yours. If it is, the previous sheet may not have been submitted, or the head referee has not loaded the next match yet.

??? question "No team is showing"
    No match is loaded for your table. Wait for the head referee.

??? question "There are connection markers in the top bar"
    Your tablet has lost the server. See [Connecting a device](../getting-started/connecting.md#the-connection-status-indicator). Keep the tablet on the scoring screen; your answers stay on the screen and you can submit when it reconnects.

??? question "The scoresheet is just a score box"
    The event is running in **Agnostic** mode, where you enter the final score yourself instead of answering questions. See [Scoring and scoresheets](../guides/scoring.md#agnostic-scoring).
