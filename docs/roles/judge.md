# I am a judge

TMS is mainly a robot game system. Judging rubrics are completed outside TMS, so a judge account has very little to do in it.

!!! abstract "Your screens"
    The public **Scoreboard** and **Timer**. A judge account does not currently unlock any further screens.

## What you can see

Anyone, logged in or not, can open the **Scoreboard** from the home screen. It shows:

- every team's **rank** and scores by round, and
- with the **Judging Schedule** box ticked, the upcoming judging sessions with the team in each pod.

That second view is the useful one for a judging room: it tells you who is due next and where.

## What you need from the judge advisor

The judge advisor is the judging contact in TMS. They:

- keep the **judging sessions** and **pods** up to date if the schedule changes,
- tick off each team's **Core Values**, **Innovation** and **Robot Design** rubrics as they are handed in, and
- can give you each team's **Gracious Professionalism** ratings and referee comments from the robot game, exported from Team Data.

If your session list looks wrong, or a team has not arrived, tell the judge advisor.

!!! note "Need more access?"
    If a judge needs to edit sessions or see team data, the event administrator can give their account the judge advisor role instead.
